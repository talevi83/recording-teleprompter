How to Use

1. Paste your script into the editor.

2. Adjust settings (text size, speed, margins, etc.).

3. Click "Start Teleprompter" to begin. The recording will start automatically after a 3-second countdown (unless Practice Mode is checked).

4. During recording, use the Spacebar to pause/resume scrolling, and Up/Down arrows to adjust speed.

5. Click "Stop & Save" to finish recording and save the video to your device.
